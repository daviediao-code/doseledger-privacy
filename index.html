DoseLedger

Privacy Policy

Your medication reminders and dose history stay on your device. No account, no ads, no analytics.

Effective date: 2026-11-20
Developer: AstraCore Studio
App: DoseLedger — Medication Reminder & Pill Log (com.astracore.doseledger)
Contact: daviediao@gmail.com

DoseLedger is a medication reminder and self-reported pill log. We designed it so that your medication data stays on your device. This policy explains what the app does with your information, and what it deliberately does not do.

1.Information we do not collect

DoseLedger does not require an account. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share your name, email address, contacts, location, advertising identifiers, or the medication information you enter. The app contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, and no crash-reporting SDK.

2.Information stored on your device

You may enter the following into the app:

  • medication names, strengths, forms, doses, units, schedules, start and end dates, stock counts and notes;
  • dose records, including scheduled times, status (pending, taken, skipped) and the time you logged a dose;
  • app preferences such as theme and reminder settings.

This information is stored in a private database and preferences file inside the app's sandbox on your device. It is not uploaded to us or to any third party by the app.

3.Android system backup

DoseLedger disables Android's automatic cloud backup and device-to-device transfer for app data. Your medication data is therefore not copied to Google Drive or transferred to another device by the operating system. To move your data, use the app's own export feature (see section 4).

4.Export and import

The app lets you export a JSON backup and, with the Pro unlock, PDF and CSV reports. These files are written only to the location you choose through the Android system file picker. Once saved, those files are outside the app's control; you are responsible for where you store or share them.

5.Permissions

  • Notifications (Android 13+): used only to show the dose reminders you configured.
  • Exact alarms (Android 12+): used to deliver reminders at the scheduled minute. If not granted, reminders may be delayed by the system.
  • Run after reboot: used to restore your reminder schedule after the device restarts.
  • Vibration and wake lock: used for reminder notifications.
  • Internet: used only by Google Play Billing to load the Pro product and process purchases.

The app does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, or broad storage permissions.

6.Purchases

DoseLedger Pro is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play. Google handles your payment method and purchase history under Google's Privacy Policy. The app receives only the product identifier, purchase state, and a purchase token needed to unlock and restore Pro. We never receive your card details.

7.Data sharing

We do not share your medication data with anyone. The only third-party processing associated with the app is Google Play Billing, as described above.

8.Retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual doses, archive or delete medications, or uninstall the app, which removes its local database and preferences. Because we never receive your data, we cannot delete or recover it for you. Export a backup first if you want to keep a copy.

9.Security

Data is protected by Android's app sandbox and your device's own lock screen and encryption. No method of storage is completely secure; please protect your device with a passcode and keep your exported backups in a safe place.

10.Children

DoseLedger is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11.Medical disclaimer

DoseLedger is a personal tracking tool, not a medical device. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, dosage recommendations, or drug-interaction checks. Always follow the instructions of your clinician or pharmacist. In an emergency, contact local emergency services.

12.Your rights

Because we do not receive your personal data, requests to access, correct, export, or delete it can be fulfilled directly in the app: view or edit records, export a backup, or uninstall the app. If you have questions about this policy, contact us at daviediao@gmail.com.

13.Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected in the app or the store listing with a new effective date. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
